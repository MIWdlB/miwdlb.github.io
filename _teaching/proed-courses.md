Since July 2023 I have taught courses for ProEd Et Al:

- **Introduction to Quantum Computing from Computer Science** - a 6 hour in-person course for university entrants.
- **Physical Sciences and Programming** - a 3 day course covering study and careers in physical and information sciences for students in late secondary education.
- **Introduction to Computer Science** - a 6 hour online course covering classical information theory, physical computing and computational science for students in secondary education.
